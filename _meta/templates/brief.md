# <subject title>

## What it is

What the subject is in two or three plain sentences: the kind of thing, for whom, to what end.

## People

One line per person: name, organisation, role, and every address, chat handle and recording speaker label seen for them.

## Names and aliases

Every name the subject goes by in records (titles, abbreviations, mail domains, project codes), each with a record that shows it.

## How it started

The first contact or decision that began it, dated, with its record ids.

## Timeline

Dated lines, oldest first, each ending with its record ids; a line newer evidence replaced moves here with its date, never dropped.

## Where it stands

The current state in a few lines, with the date it was true; status pages are rebuilt from this, never signed.

## Sensitive

Prices, terms, ownership and people's private situations: known here so nothing contradicts them, never placed on a page.

## Open questions

What research could not answer, each with the searches tried and the best guess; a question to the owner only when a page cannot be written without it.

## Research log

One line per search: where (this space, a mount, which connector), the terms, and what it found (record ids exported, or nothing new).
